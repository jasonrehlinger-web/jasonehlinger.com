## Financial Coaching

Positioned as accessible — lower first-session cost, then an easy per-session rate for people who keep going.

| Offering | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free intro call | $0 | 30 min |
| Intake session | **$150** | First working session (45 min). Deep dive into your numbers + a plan. |
| Follow-up session | **$100** | Each 45-min session after intake. |
| 6-Session Package | **$550** | Intake + 5 follow-ups. Save $100 vs. $650 pay-as-you-go. |

**Why this structure:** the $150 intake covers the heavier first-session work; $100 keeps ongoing sessions easy to say yes to. The package gives committed clients a reason to buy ahead and saves you the re-selling every session.

## Leadership Coaching

Priced slightly above finance to reflect executive positioning, per your call.

| Offering | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free intro call | $0 | 30 min |
| Single session | **$175** | Per 45-min session, pay as you go. |
| 6-Session Package | **$900** | Save $150 vs. $1,050. Works out to $150/session. |

**Why this structure:** a flat $175 keeps it simple. The 6-session package is where most real leadership change happens (it takes more than one conversation), and the ~14% discount rewards that commitment.
